Digital Person – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Digital Person is a private, non-commercial personal finance app. It is built and used by its owner and members of the owner's household to keep track of their own spending. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app handles

With the account holder's explicit consent, given on their bank's own pages, the app reads account information for that person's own payment accounts: the list of accounts, balances and transactions. The app never sees or stores bank login credentials.

How the data is obtained

Account information is retrieved through Enable Banking Oy, a registered Account Information Service Provider. Consent is given for a limited period, can be withdrawn at any time in the bank's settings or in the app, and has to be renewed after it expires.

Where the data is stored

All account information is stored only on the device where the app is installed. There is no server operated for this app, no cloud database and no analytics. The key used to sign requests to Enable Banking is kept in the device keychain.

Who the data is shared with

Nobody. The data is not sold, shared or transferred to any third party. The app also downloads public currency exchange rates from Narodowy Bank Polski; no personal or account data is sent in that request.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until the user deletes it. It can be erased at any time from the app's settings, or by removing the app. Disconnecting a bank in the app also closes the corresponding consent.

This page

The page at the address registered as the app's redirect URL only passes the one-time authorisation code back to the app on the same device. It stores nothing and loads no third-party scripts.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app's owner through the GitHub account that hosts this page.
