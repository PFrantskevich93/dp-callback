Digital Person – Terms of Service

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What this is

Digital Person is a private, non-commercial personal finance app. It is used only by its owner and members of the owner's household, with their own bank accounts. It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.

What the app does

The app shows the user their own account transactions and summarises them by day, month, account and category. It only reads account information. It cannot make payments or move money.

Consent

Access to an account is possible only after its holder approves it on their bank's own pages. That consent is limited in time and can be withdrawn at any time, in the bank's settings or by disconnecting the bank in the app.

No warranty

The app is provided as is, for personal use. Its figures are for information only and are not financial, tax or accounting advice. Bank statements remain the authoritative record.
